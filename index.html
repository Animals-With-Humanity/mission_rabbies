Why this drive matters

A few facts worth knowing about rabies

Rabies is almost always fatal

Once symptoms appear, there is no cure - but it is 100% preventable with a timely vaccine.

rabies are transmissible

Most human rabies cases come from dog bites. Vaccinating dogs protects entire neighbourhoods.

One shot, long protection

A single anti-rabies vaccine gives your community dog strong protection and helps stop the disease from spreading.

It protects people too

Community dogs live close to families - vaccinating them keeps children and neighbours safe.

Before you register - please note

Common Questions

Is this really 100% free?

Yes - the anti-rabies vaccine is completely free. The only paid add-on is the optional 9-in-1 vaccine, and only if you choose it.

Do I need to book a specific time slot?

No fixed slot needed. Once you register, our team will contact you on WhatsApp or call to confirm the date and time for your area.

My dog is aggressive or scared of strangers - can I still register?

Yes. Just mark the temperament honestly in the form - our trained handlers know how to safely vaccinate nervous or aggressive dogs.

Why does my dog need to be dewormed first?

A dewormed dog builds stronger immunity from the vaccine. Use the dosage table above based on your community dog's weight, a few days before the drive.

Can I register more than one dog?

Absolutely - just enter the total number of dogs in the form and we'll vaccinate all of them during the visit.

Will the vaccination hurt my dog?

It's a quick, routine injection - like any vaccine, dogs may feel a brief pinch but recover within minutes with no lasting discomfort.
